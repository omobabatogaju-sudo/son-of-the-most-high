KSRV Feasibility Framework

TURNING THE IDEA INTO A REAL PROJECT.

This is the KSRV project-development framework for testing whether the 100-student pilot can move from concept to a viable site, cost plan, operating model and partnership structure.

Prepared for partnership and feasibility conversations — September 2026.

More than a 100-bed hostel.

KSRV must prove that a student village can combine accommodation, learning, services, recreation, transport and enterprise while remaining financially sustainable and operationally manageable.

04 / Site + Land

The land must support a village, not just buildings.

Our current planning target is about 3–5 acres. The final requirement must come from the approved master plan and site-specific planning conditions.

Site testWhat KSRV must confirm
LocationAccess to the intended campus cluster and urban services.
TitleClean, verifiable ownership and transferable/financeable interest.
PlanningCompatible land use and a credible approval route.
AccessAll-weather road access, safe entrance and service access.
UtilitiesPractical route for electricity, water, telecoms and drainage.
Flood / drainageFlood risk, natural drainage and stormwater requirements.
Soil / geologyGeotechnical investigation before structural cost is fixed.
ExpansionPotential for future phases where demand and financing support them.

06 / Construction Cost Scenarios

Use scenarios, not a fake exact budget.

Published 2026 Nigerian construction estimates vary significantly. KSRV should not lock its investor case to a generic internet number.

Lower planning case

₦250k/m²

Illustrative base build on 4,020 m²: ₦1.005bn.

Middle planning case

₦350k/m²

Illustrative base build on 4,020 m²: ₦1.407bn.

Upper planning case

₦450k/m²

Illustrative base build on 4,020 m²: ₦1.809bn.

Important: these are planning scenarios only. A 20% allowance for professional fees, statutory/development costs, external works and contingency would move the illustrative ranges to about ₦1.206bn, ₦1.689bn and ₦2.171bn respectively. Land and financing costs are excluded. A project-specific QS cost plan/BOQ must replace these assumptions.

Current references include 2026 Nigerian construction-cost guides and recent quantity-surveyor commentary, which show broad market variation by specification, location, soil and scope.

07 / Operating + Affordability

Affordable to students. Sustainable to operate.

The financial model needs to separate recurring accommodation revenue from supporting service and enterprise revenue.

Revenue / support channelRole in model
AccommodationCore recurring revenue; fee bands should follow cost, demand, occupancy and affordability analysis.
Utilities / service chargesTransparent recovery of defined services where appropriate.
Student enterpriseVendor/space arrangements and service commissions designed to be fair and sustainable.
Laundry / printing / mini-martEveryday service and convenience revenue.
CBT / training / eventsAncillary revenue from spaces that can serve residents and external users.
ShuttlePotential transport pass or integrated accommodation/service model.
Partnership / sponsorshipTargeted support for learning, enterprise, transport, technology or amenities.
Local benchmark: In July 2026, the University of Ilorin reported approved accommodation fees of ₦204,000 for four-person rooms and ₦255,000 for two-person rooms in privately owned hostels on the university premises, plus a ₦36,400 utility charge. These are benchmarks for context, not KSRV prices.

Source: University of Ilorin, July 2026.

Faith foundation: KSRV is Christ-centered without being a church compound. Christian faith shapes values and culture; optional fellowship, prayer and Bible study can exist for students who choose to participate.

10 / Decision Gates

Do not build until the evidence is ready.

Each gate should answer a different question before the next major commitment.

GATE 1

Site

Title, access, planning, utilities, drainage and acquisition terms are credible.

GATE 2

Design

Master plan, room programme, floor areas and technical strategy are defined.

GATE 3

Cost

QS cost plan, professional fees, external works and contingency are quantified.

GATE 4

Market

Student demand, institutions, competition, affordability and shuttle demand are tested.

GATE 5

Operations

Staffing, maintenance, utilities, security, enterprise and digital systems are viable.

GATE 6

Funding

Capital structure, investor/finance terms and land contribution are defined.

GATE 7

Approvals

Planning, building and other required approvals have a clear route.

GATE 8

Build

Final design, procurement route, contracts, budget and risk controls are ready.
