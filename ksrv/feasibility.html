Pre-Feasibility Cost Envelope

Plan with a range, not a fake exact number.

A 2026 published Nigerian residential construction guide reports ₦120,000–₦230,000/m² for standard residential construction. KSRV is more infrastructure-intensive than a standard house, so the planning model uses a higher blended allowance of ₦200,000–₦300,000/m². That higher KSRV allowance is an internal planning assumption and must be replaced by a project-specific BoQ.

Accommodation Economics

Current local rates give us a benchmark — not a final KSRV price.

In July 2026, the University of Ilorin said approved accommodation fees for privately owned hostels on campus were ₦204,000 for four-person rooms and ₦255,000 for two-person rooms, plus a ₦36,400 utility charge for essential services. KSRV should set final fees only after its cost, demand and service-level study.

What the numbers tell the project team

The 100-student pilot should not be financed like a simple 100-bed hostel. The concept includes learning space, village services, recreation, transport and infrastructure. The project therefore needs a blended capital strategy and phased construction rather than assuming room fees alone will fund everything.

Potential routes include land contribution or long lease, patient equity, long-tenor development finance, strategic corporate support, institutional partnerships and carefully structured student-service revenues.
