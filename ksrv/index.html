Accommodation

Four residential blocks. Four ways to live.

Shared does not mean cramped. The room strategy starts with dignity, adequate personal space and consistent quality.

BLOCK A

4-Person

32 students
  • 8 spacious shared units
  • 4 beds + 4 wardrobes + 4 desks
  • Shared lounge/study zone
  • Generous bathroom provision
BLOCK B

2-Person

24 students
  • 12 two-person units
  • Distinct personal zones
  • Shared living/study area
  • Private bathroom target
BLOCK C

Private

16 students
  • 16 private units
  • Own sleeping space
  • Study desk + storage
  • Privacy-focused accommodation
BLOCK D

Mixed

28 students
  • 4 four-person units
  • 4 two-person units
  • 4 private units
  • Flexible accommodation mix

These are project-planning figures. Final dimensions and technical requirements will be determined through qualified architectural, engineering and statutory work.

Student Enterprise Program

Students should graduate with more than a certificate.

A resident with a useful skill or business idea should be able to operate a properly managed service inside KSRV while studying.

Selection can consider academic standing, good conduct, capability, service quality and the viability of the idea. CGPA can be part of the process, not the only measure.
