KSRV - Kwara Student Resident Village

PROJECT BRIEF

More than a place to stay. A place to become.

This brief presents the working concept for KSRV Phase 1, the 100-student pilot village, and the partnership framework needed to move from concept to feasibility and delivery.

← KSRV   Feasibility   Master Plan
PHASE 1: 100 STUDENTS • 4 RESIDENTIAL BLOCKS • 48 ACCOMMODATION UNITS
01 / Vision

A student village built around the whole student experience.

KSRV is a proposed student residential community in Kwara State combining accommodation, learning, everyday services, transport, recreation, enterprise and community in one coordinated environment.

Live

Comfortable accommodation, reliable utilities, security and green space.

Learn

Library, study rooms, CBT/computer facilities and digital learning.

Connect

Clubs, events, recreation, student council and community life.

Grow

Student enterprise, mentorship, skills and practical experience.

02 / Pilot Snapshot

100 students. One complete pilot village.

The first phase is intentionally manageable so the model can be built, operated, tested and improved before expansion.

03 / Residential Buildings

Four blocks. Different living options.

Shared does not mean cramped. The room strategy starts with dignity, adequate personal space and consistent quality.

BlockTypeUnitsStudentsPlanning concept
A4-person832Spacious shared apartments with 4 beds, 4 wardrobes, 4 desks, shared lounge/study and generous bathroom provision as a planning target.
B2-person1224Two-person apartments with distinct personal zones, shared living/study area and private bathroom target.
CPrivate1616Individual accommodation focused on privacy, study, storage and comfort.
DMixed12284 four-person + 4 two-person + 4 private units.
Planning note: room sizes, structure, fire safety, accessibility, drainage, plumbing and other technical requirements remain subject to professional architectural and engineering design.

06 / Student Enterprise

Students should graduate with more than a certificate.

KSRV can provide managed spaces where students operate small businesses and services while studying. Selection can consider academic standing, good conduct, practical capability, service quality, safety/hygiene and idea viability. CGPA can be one factor, not the only factor.

Food

Student-run meal services.

Laundry

Washing, ironing and delivery.

Printing

Mini-Mart

Grooming

Barbering and personal care.

Creative

Photography, design, media and content.

Tutoring

Peer academic support.

07 / Student Shuttle

Campus should not become a daily transport battle.

The KSRV concept includes a dedicated shuttle system connecting the village with participating campuses on scheduled routes.

Scheduled Routes

Morning, afternoon and evening service can be planned around actual demand.

Safe Operations

Properly maintained vehicles, trained drivers and clear procedures.

Digital Updates

Residents can see schedules and future service information through the KSRV platform.

08 / Community + Values

Christ-centered in foundation. Student-centered in experience.

KSRV is not intended to be a church compound. Christian faith shapes the project's values and culture while the village remains a welcoming student community.

Integrity

Honest management and responsible stewardship.

Excellence

Careful design, quality service and continuous improvement.

Service

Using skills and opportunities to serve others.

Respect

Peaceful community life and dignity for residents.

Stewardship

Care for people, facilities, environment and future.

Faith

Optional Christian fellowship, prayer and Bible study for students who choose to participate.

10 / Development Roadmap

Start responsibly. Scale intentionally.

StageFocus
Phase 1 - 100Four residential blocks, learning facilities, village centre, infrastructure, shuttle and community systems.
Phase 2 - 250Expand accommodation and strengthen enterprise, learning, recreation and services.
Phase 3 - 500Develop a larger student community with additional amenities and operational depth.
Long term - 1,000+Scale the model where land, demand, operating performance and financing support it.
Sustainability approach: accommodation fees can be complemented by carefully structured service, enterprise and facility revenues. Exact pricing should follow a real feasibility and operating model.
