KSRV Phase 1

MASTER SITE PLAN

Working 4-acre planning model for the 100-student pilot village. The final site plan will be produced from a real survey, site constraints, planning controls and professional design.

01 / Master Plan Idea

Build a village, not four hostel blocks.

The intended daily flow is arrival → home → learning → food/services → recreation → community → shuttle → campus. Buildings are arranged around a central green heart so the project feels calm, connected and human.

03 / Building Placement

Every building has a reason for where it sits.

Residential Zone

Blocks A–D are arranged around the central green, with quieter private accommodation separated from high-traffic areas.

Learning Centre

Placed between residences and the village centre so students can reach study facilities without crossing service traffic.

Village Centre

Near the residential zone and visible enough to support everyday activity, but separated from the quietest private rooms.

Admin / Arrival

Near the entrance so visitors, partners and students can find reception without entering the residential core.

Recreation

At the edge of the site to contain activity noise and protect the calm of the central village.

Utilities

On a service edge with screened access for maintenance, waste and infrastructure operations.

04 / Movement

Service vehiclesBack-of-house route for food, waste, maintenance and utilities where practical.

05 / Central Green

The green heart of KSRV.

The central green is not leftover land. It is the visual and social heart of the village.

Shade

Tree canopy and covered seating for thermal comfort.

Study

Outdoor tables and charging points where practical.

Community

Small events, student gatherings and informal meetings.

Movement

Walking routes connecting the main buildings.
