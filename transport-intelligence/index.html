How the pilot works

A simple loop: observe, report, decide.
3 steps
01 / CHECK
Choose your stop
Select New Park or Oke Odo and review reports for that location.
02 / CONTRIBUTE
Share what you see
Submit the queue, vehicles, route, and fare if you know them.
03 / DECIDE
Use your judgement
Check report age and confirm current fares and availability before moving.

What this pilot can and cannot do

Transparent by design, so students know what to trust.
✓ Available now in this demo
Location selection, report submission, local report history, timestamps, fare reference, and shareable page.
↗ Needed for public launch
Shared database across phones, moderation, report rate-limits, official fare confirmation, and student testing at both stops.
UNILORIN Transport Intelligence
A student-led pilot concept. This is not an official University of Ilorin or Students’ Union service. Reports currently remain in this browser on this device; other students cannot see them yet. A shared database and moderation process are required before public launch.
